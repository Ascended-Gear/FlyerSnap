Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 17, 2026

Ascended Gear ("we," "our," or "us") built FlyerSnap as a privacy-first utility. Your privacy is paramount: we do not collect, transmit, track, or sell any of your personal information.

🔒 Zero Data Collection: All text extraction, photo processing, and calendar events are handled 100% locally on your iPhone or iPad. No data ever leaves your device.

1. On-Device Text Recognition (OCR)

FlyerSnap uses Apple's native Vision framework to recognize text from paper flyers, digital graphics, and PDF documents. All processing runs entirely on your device's Apple Neural Engine. Photos and scans are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud service.

2. Device Permissions Used

  • Camera: Used exclusively to photograph physical flyers, posters, and notices within the app.
  • Photo Library: Used solely to allow you to select flyer screenshots and invitation images from your library.
  • Calendar: FlyerSnap requests write-only access to your Apple Calendar to schedule events with custom prep alerts. The app cannot read or inspect your existing calendar events.

3. Analytics & Third-Party Tracking

FlyerSnap contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising frameworks, and no tracking pixels. We do not track your location, your device identifiers, or your usage habits.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases and upgrades are processed directly through Apple's App Store via StoreKit 2. We do not receive or process your credit card numbers or billing details; transactions are subject to Apple's standard privacy practices.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do I add a flyer to my calendar?
    Snap a photo or import an image, verify the detected dates and venue, and tap "Save & Add to Calendar."
  • Can I zoom in to inspect fine print?
    Yes! Tap any flyer preview to open the interactive viewer with pinch-to-zoom (up to 6x), double-tap zoom, and pan gestures.
  • How do multi-day events work?
    FlyerSnap supports both continuous date ranges and individual multi-day sessions (e.g., Day 1, Day 2, Day 3). All sessions are synced to your Calendar.
  • Can I scan multi-page PDFs?
    Yes, tap "Import File" on the capture screen to select PDF announcements or schedules from the Files app.
  • How do I restore my Pro purchase?
    Navigate to Settings in the app and tap "Restore Purchases."
